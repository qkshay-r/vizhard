### WIP

/vizhard: Welcomes you in, asks what point you want to make and who it is for, and directs the other workers.  
/barber: Cleans the messy spreadsheet by cutting out useless data, fixing broken symbols, and tidying up numbers.  
/cobbler: Decides how each piece of information should actually be shown, like picking between position, bar length, or color.  
/carpenter: Makes big numbers relatable by turning them into everyday comparisons, flattening extreme spikes, or contrasting them against shocking facts.  
/tailor: Styles the graphic with good-looking fonts, balanced colors, and neat spacing, then writes the display code.  
/apprentice-tailor: Searches online for real-world design examples, color codes, and trending styles to inspire the tailor.  
/critique: Quality-checks the draft for visual clutter, confusing layouts, or misleading charts, and orders fixes if needed.  
/janitor: Cleans up behind the scenes by deleting leftover temporary files, removing unused code, and making sure everything runs smoothly.
